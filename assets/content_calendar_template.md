## 2. Content pillars

Use a mix that reflects the audience and the campaign. The suggested 40/25/25/10 split is a planning starting point, not a rule.

| Pillar | Share | Purpose | Topics |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Educational | 40% | Teach, explain, solve audience problems | |
| Trust and proof | 25% | Demonstrate expertise and evidence | |
| Product or service | 25% | Explain value, use cases, and differentiation | |
| Community or culture | 10% | Humanize the brand and invite participation | |

## 4. Production checklist

- [ ] Audience and intent are defined.
- [ ] The content has one primary objective.
- [ ] Claims are supported by reliable evidence.
- [ ] Brand voice is consistent.
- [ ] Headline and opening communicate the value clearly.
- [ ] Links and calls to action work.
- [ ] Images have appropriate rights and descriptive alt text.
- [ ] Mobile layout and readability have been checked.
- [ ] Legal, product, and subject-matter reviews are complete where needed.
- [ ] Final URL and publication date are recorded.
