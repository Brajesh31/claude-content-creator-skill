# Social Media Optimization Guide

Use this guide to adapt content to each platform and audience. Platform features, limits, formats, and ranking systems change; verify current specifications in the platform's own creator or business documentation before production.

## 1. Start with the audience

For each channel, document:
- target audience and their current level of awareness;
- the problem or interest that brings them to the platform;
- the content format they are likely to consume;
- the action the content should encourage;
- the metric that represents meaningful progress.

Do not select a platform only because it is popular. Choose channels that match the audience and the team's ability to publish consistently.

## 2. Platform adaptation

| Channel | Strong content patterns | Optimization focus |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Professional lessons, industry analysis, project stories, useful documents, founder perspectives | A clear first line, specific insight, credible evidence, meaningful discussion |
| Instagram | Reels, carousels, visual storytelling, creator-led explanations | Strong visual opening, legible text, accessible captions, coherent visual identity |
| YouTube | Tutorials, explainers, reviews, interviews, long-form education, Shorts | Accurate title and thumbnail, clear viewer promise, strong opening, chapters where useful |
| TikTok | Native short video, demonstrations, commentary, creator-led education | Immediate context, authentic delivery, concise editing, relevant captions |
| X | Timely commentary, concise analysis, threads, product or research updates | One idea per post, useful context, accurate claims, meaningful conversation |
| Facebook | Community posts, local information, video, groups, event updates | Audience relevance, conversation, accessible creative, clear event or offer details |

These are common patterns, not guarantees of reach or performance.

## 3. Creative checklist

### Opening
- Lead with a problem, observation, question, result, or useful contrast.
- Make the subject clear without requiring the reader to expand the post.
- Avoid misleading hooks that the content does not fulfill.

### Body
- Keep one primary idea.
- Use concrete examples and short sections.
- Remove filler and repeated claims.
- Explain unfamiliar terms.
- Keep promotional claims proportionate to the evidence.

### Visuals
- Use original or properly licensed assets.
- Keep text readable on mobile.
- Maintain contrast and avoid relying on color alone.
- Add captions to spoken video.
- Provide meaningful alternative text where supported.
- Avoid excessive text overlays and decorative clutter.

### Call to action
- Ask for one relevant next step.
- Do not manufacture engagement bait.
- Ensure the linked destination matches the promise of the post.

## 4. Hashtags, keywords, and metadata

- Use terms your audience actually uses.
- Add hashtags only when they improve discovery or context.
- Avoid long blocks of unrelated hashtags.
- Use descriptive titles and captions.
- Keep keywords natural; do not stuff them into copy.
- Verify platform-specific tagging and metadata behavior before scheduling.

## 5. Repurposing workflow

1. Identify the strongest idea in the source asset.
2. Choose a platform-specific format.
3. Rewrite the hook for the destination audience.
4. Shorten or expand the explanation as needed.
5. Rework the visual and CTA.
6. Check facts and rights again.
7. Record the source asset for measurement and attribution.

## 6. Measurement

Measure outcomes against the objective.

| Objective | Useful indicators |
|---|---|
| Awareness | Qualified reach, relevant impressions, video retention |
| Engagement | Meaningful comments, saves, shares, completion |
| Traffic | Link clicks, landing-page sessions, engaged sessions |
| Leads | Qualified form submissions, demo requests, sign-ups |
| Revenue | Attributed conversions, pipeline contribution, revenue |

Do not compare raw engagement counts across channels without considering audience size, format, and objective.

## 7. Testing

Change one major variable at a time when practical:
- opening hook;
- creative format;
- message angle;
- CTA;
- audience segment;
- publishing time.

Record the hypothesis, observation window, result, and next decision. Do not declare a winner from tiny samples or one unusually successful post.

## 8. Publishing checklist

- [ ] Platform and audience are defined.
- [ ] Format matches the channel.
- [ ] Hook accurately reflects the content.
- [ ] Creative is readable and accessible.
- [ ] Claims and links are checked.
- [ ] Assets are original or licensed.
- [ ] CTA is clear.
- [ ] Current platform specifications were verified.
- [ ] Measurement objective is recorded.
